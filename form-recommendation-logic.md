A good recommendation flow shouldn't start with **Arabica vs Robusta**—most consumers don't know what those mean. Instead, ask about the experience they want, then map those answers to the coffee.

Here's a recommendation tree you could use.

---

# Coffee Finder

### 1. How do you usually drink your coffee?

* ☕ Black
* 🥛 With milk
* 🔄 Both

---

## If Black

### 2. What kind of flavor do you enjoy?

* 🍓 Fruity & bright
* 🍫 Chocolatey & smooth
* 🌰 Nutty & balanced
* 🌍 Rich & earthy
* 🤷 I'm not sure

---

### 3. How much bitterness do you like?

* Almost none
* A little
* Medium
* Strong

---

### 4. Why do you drink coffee?

* I enjoy the taste
* I need energy
* Both

---

### 5. How much effort do you want?

* Just add hot water (Instant)
* 2–3 minutes (French Press)
* 4–5 minutes (Pour Over)
* I don't mind

---

## If With Milk

### 2. How strong should it taste through milk?

* Mild
* Balanced
* Strong
* Very strong

---

### 3. What flavors do you like?

* Chocolate
* Caramel
* Nuts
* Fruity
* Not sure

---

### 4. Why do you drink coffee?

* Taste
* Energy
* Both

---

### 5. How much effort?

* Instant
* French Press
* Espresso Machine
* Moka Pot
* Anything easy

---

# Recommendation Logic

## If

* Black
* Fruity
* Very low bitterness
* Drinks for taste

Recommend:

> **100% Arabica – Light Roast – Washed Process**

Characteristics:

* Citrus
* Berry
* Floral
* Tea-like
* Low bitterness

---

## If

* Black
* Chocolate
* Medium bitterness

Recommend:

> **100% Arabica – Medium Roast**

Characteristics:

* Chocolate
* Caramel
* Brown sugar
* Balanced acidity

---

## If

* Black
* Earthy
* Strong bitterness
* Drinks for energy

Recommend:

> **Arabica–Robusta Blend (70:30 or 60:40)**

Characteristics:

* Bold
* Heavy body
* Higher caffeine

---

## If

* Milk
* Wants café style

Recommend:

> **Medium-Dark Arabica**

Characteristics:

* Chocolate
* Caramel
* Hazelnut

Works well with milk.

---

## If

* Milk
* Wants very strong coffee

Recommend:

> **50–70% Robusta Blend**

Characteristics:

* Thick body
* High caffeine
* Punches through milk

---

## If

* Drinks coffee mainly for caffeine

Ask one extra question:

### "How much caffeine are you looking for?"

* Moderate
* High
* Maximum

Then:

Moderate
→ 100% Arabica

High
→ 80:20 Arabica–Robusta

Maximum
→ 50:50 Blend or High-Robusta

---

## Brewing Method Logic

If they choose **lowest effort**:

Recommend:

* Instant Coffee
* Specialty Instant (freeze-dried)
* Coffee bags

---

If they can spend **2–3 minutes**:

Recommend:

* French Press
* AeroPress

---

If they enjoy making coffee:

Recommend:

* V60
* Chemex
* Espresso

---

# Final Recommendation Engine

Instead of saying:

> "You should buy Arabica."

Present a personalized result like this:

---

### Based on your answers

**We think you'll enjoy:**

✅ 100% Arabica
✅ Medium Roast
✅ Washed Process
✅ Ground for French Press

### Why?

You told us that you:

* Prefer coffee with milk
* Enjoy chocolate and caramel flavors
* Don't like excessive bitterness
* Want moderate caffeine
* Prefer a quick brew

This coffee delivers a smooth, balanced cup with notes of milk chocolate and caramel, enough body to stand up to milk, and moderate caffeine without the harsh bitterness of higher-Robusta blends.

---

This kind of recommendation feels far more personal than simply asking "Arabica or Robusta" and guides users toward a coffee that matches their preferences rather than their familiarity with coffee terminology.
