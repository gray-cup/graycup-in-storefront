Most people add sugar to coffee to cover bitterness. The fix isn't willpower - it's choosing coffee that isn't bitter to begin with. The right beans, roast and brew give you a cup that's naturally sweet-ish, smooth and easy to drink with nothing added.

## Why Some Black Coffee Needs Sugar

- **Too much Robusta.** Robusta is roughly twice as bitter as Arabica.
- **Over-roasted.** Dark, oily roasts add scorched, ashy bitterness.
- **Over-extracted.** Water too hot, grind too fine, or brewed too long pulls harsh compounds.
- **Stale.** Old coffee tastes flat and papery, which pushes people toward sugar.

Fix those and sugar becomes optional.

## How to Brew It Sweeter (Without Sugar)

- **Water at 90-94 C**, not boiling.
- **Grind a little coarser** if it tastes bitter.
- **Start at 1:16** coffee to water; too strong reads as bitter.
- **Drink it slightly cooler** - very hot coffee tastes more bitter.
- **Grind fresh.** Stale grounds are the most common reason a cup "needs" sugar.
