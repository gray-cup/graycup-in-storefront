Indian Arabica is grown at altitude under shade, mostly in Karnataka, Tamil Nadu, Kerala, Odisha and the Northeast, and mostly washed or honey-processed. The result is a clean, aromatic, medium-bodied cup with more acidity and less bitterness than Robusta - the bean to choose for black coffee, pour over and French press.

## What to Look For

- **Single origin or single estate** if you want to taste where it's from.
- **Washed process** for clarity, **honey/natural** for more sweetness and body.
- **Medium roast** to keep the origin character.
- **A roast date on the bag** - Arabica's aromatics fade fast.

## Brewing Arabica

Arabica rewards care: filtered water at 90-94 C, a fresh medium grind, a 1:16 ratio, and a 3-4 minute brew. Over-hot water or too-fine a grind buries the delicate notes you paid for.
