Yes - for most people, black coffee is a genuinely good pre-workout, and it's the ingredient most commercial pre-workouts are built around anyway. Caffeine is the one component with strong, repeated evidence for improving training. A cup of coffee delivers it just as well as a scoop of powder, for a fraction of the cost and with no proprietary blend to decode.

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## What Coffee Covers

- **Caffeine** - improves endurance, power output, repeated efforts, and lowers perceived exertion. This is 80% of what a pre-workout does.
- **Cheap and simple** - a few rupees a cup, no dosing guesswork.
- **Timing control** - drink it 45-60 minutes before training so it peaks as you start.

## What Coffee Doesn't Cover

Some pre-workout ingredients have their own (smaller) evidence base and aren't in coffee:

- **Creatine** - worth taking, but it works from being taken daily, not pre-workout specifically.
- **Beta-alanine** - modest help for 1-4 minute efforts; causes the tingling.
- **Citrulline** - possible small pump/endurance effect.

If you want those, take them separately - you don't need a blended tub, and coffee still handles the caffeine.
