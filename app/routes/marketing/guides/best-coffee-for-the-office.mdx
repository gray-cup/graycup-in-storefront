Most office coffee is a vending machine or a jar of stale instant left open since last quarter. Bringing your own takes almost no effort and turns the worst cup of your day into a decent one.

## Three Easy Office Setups

- **Steel South Indian filter** - make a decoction at home or at your desk, add hot water or milk from the pantry.
- **French press / plunger** - coarse grounds, hot water, four minutes, done at your desk.
- **A good instant** - genuinely fine for travel and offices with no kettle time; keep it for that.

## What to Bring

- **A forgiving medium-dark blend** that tastes fine even if your water isn't quite hot enough.
- **Small airtight container** - don't leave the whole bag open in a drawer.
- **Your own ratio** worked out once (~1:15) so every cup is consistent.

## Timing at Work

A cup mid-to-late morning and a small one after lunch covers the two natural energy dips. Skip anything after ~3-4 pm so it doesn't follow you home.
