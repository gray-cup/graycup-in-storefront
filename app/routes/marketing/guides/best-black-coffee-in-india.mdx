"Best black coffee" really means one question: which coffee tastes good with nothing added? A lot of coffee is roasted dark and blended heavy on Robusta so it can stand up to milk and sugar. Drink that black and it's harsh. The coffees below are chosen the other way round - smooth, aromatic, low enough in bitterness to enjoy plain.

India grows excellent coffee for this: shade-grown Arabica from Karnataka, Odisha and the Northeast, mostly washed or honey-processed, with clean cups that don't need hiding.

## How to Brew Black Coffee

Pour over, French press and South Indian filter all work. Use fresh grounds, water just off the boil (90-96 C), and a 1:15 to 1:16 ratio for a standard cup - stronger if you like. Grind fresh if you can; it's the single biggest quality upgrade.
