Decaf coffee has had most - not all - of its caffeine removed before roasting. A cup of decaf still has roughly 2-5 mg of caffeine, versus 80-150+ mg in regular coffee. It's for people who like the ritual and taste of coffee but need to avoid caffeine, in the evening or entirely.

## How Decaffeination Works

- **Swiss Water / Mountain Water process** - uses only water and carbon filters, no solvents. The cleanest method, and what most specialty decaf uses.
- **CO2 process** - pressurised carbon dioxide extracts the caffeine. Also solvent-free.
- **Solvent processes (methylene chloride / ethyl acetate)** - effective and considered safe by regulators, but many buyers prefer to avoid them.

Look for "Swiss Water" or "CO2 decaffeinated" on the bag if the method matters to you.

## Does Decaf Taste Worse?

Older decaf did. Modern decaf, well roasted and fresh, is close to regular - slightly less aromatic and a touch flatter, but perfectly good. Freshness matters even more with decaf, since it's already at a flavour disadvantage.

Chicory has no caffeine, so this blend delivers noticeably less per cup than a straight coffee.
