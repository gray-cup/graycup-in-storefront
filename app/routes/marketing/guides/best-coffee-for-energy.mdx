Coffee gives you energy by blocking adenosine, the molecule that builds up through the day and makes you feel tired. How much lift you get depends on the dose (caffeine content), the brew (concentration) and your own tolerance. For all-day energy you want enough caffeine to matter, spread across 2-3 cups rather than one huge hit.

The figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## Getting Steady Energy, Not a Spike

- **Space it out.** One cup on waking, one mid-morning, one after lunch. Avoid a single mega-dose.
- **Stop by early afternoon.** Caffeine has a ~5-hour half-life; an evening cup still costs you sleep, which drains tomorrow's energy.
- **Eat something.** Coffee on a totally empty stomach can spike then crash you. Pair it with breakfast.
- **Hydrate.** Mild dehydration feels like fatigue and coffee won't fix it.
