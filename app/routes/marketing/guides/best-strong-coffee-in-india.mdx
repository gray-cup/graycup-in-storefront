"Strong" can mean three different things: high caffeine, bold bitter flavour, or a concentrated brew. Usually people want all three. All three come from the same levers:

- **Bean:** Robusta - about 2,400 mg caffeine per 100 g vs Arabica's ~1,300 mg, plus more bitterness and body.
- **Roast:** dark (Vienna, French) - roasty, intense, low acidity.
- **Brew:** more grounds, less water, longer contact, no dilution.

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose per cup.

## How to Brew It Stronger

- Use a **1:10 to 1:12** ratio instead of 1:16.
- Brew a **concentrate** and drink it neat or barely diluted.
- **Longer contact:** a full 4-minute press or a slow drip beats a quick one.
- For **filter coffee**, make a thick decoction and use less water and milk.

## Know the Ceiling

About 400 mg of caffeine a day is the common safe limit for healthy adults - roughly 3-4 strong cups. Past that it's jitters and bad sleep, not more strength.
