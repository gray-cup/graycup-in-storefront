If your morning coffee doesn't do much any more, your body has adapted. With regular use, the brain grows more adenosine receptors, so the same dose blocks a smaller fraction of them. The alertness boost fades; what's left is mostly "not feeling withdrawal."

## Do You Need a Full Detox?

Not necessarily. You have three options, from gentlest to most effective:

### 1. Cut back, don't quit

Drop to one cup a day, in the late morning only. Partial tolerance reverses in about a week, and you keep enough caffeine to avoid bad withdrawal.

### 2. Cycle it

Keep intake low on rest days or weekends so the "on" days hit harder. Common with people who want caffeine to work as a pre-workout.

### 3. Full reset (7-12 days off)

The most complete. Expect 2-4 days of headaches, fatigue, low mood and poor focus, easing after day 4-5. Receptor density returns toward baseline within 1-2 weeks. Taper down over a few days first to soften the withdrawal.

## Coming Back

Restart at a *lower* dose than before - one cup, mid-morning - and hold it there. The point of resetting is wasted if you immediately climb back to four cups.
