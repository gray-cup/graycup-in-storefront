Night shifts fight your body clock, and caffeine is the main tool to stay safe and alert - especially in the 3-5 am window when the circadian drive to sleep is strongest. The skill is dosing enough to function without so much that you can't sleep when your shift ends.

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## A Night-Shift Caffeine Plan

- **Start of shift:** one strong cup.
- **Every 3-4 hours:** a smaller cup, tapering down.
- **Hard stop 3-4 hours before your planned sleep.** Caffeine's half-life is 5-6 hours; drink at 6 am and you won't sleep at 9.
- **Coffee nap:** if you get a break, drink a cup then nap 15-20 minutes - you wake as it kicks in. Most effective single trick for shift work.

## After the Shift

Wear sunglasses on the way home so daylight doesn't reset your clock, keep the bedroom dark, and accept that shift-work sleep is never quite as good. Pay it back on days off.
