If you're moving from instant to real coffee, the goal is a cup that's smooth, hard to get wrong, and pleasant with or without milk. That means a medium roast, an Arabica-forward blend, and a simple brew method. Save the bright, funky single origins for later.

## Start Here

- **Roast:** medium or medium-dark - forgiving, sweet, low bitterness.
- **Bean:** Arabica or high-Arabica blend. Skip heavy Robusta at first; it's harsh.
- **Brew:** South Indian steel filter, French press, or a simple drip machine.
- **Grind:** buy it ground to match your method if you don't have a grinder yet.

## Beginner Mistakes to Avoid

- **Boiling water.** Let it sit 30 seconds off the boil (90-94 C).
- **Old pre-ground coffee.** Buy small, fresh bags and finish them within a few weeks.
- **Guessing the ratio.** Weigh it once: ~1:15 to 1:16 coffee to water.
- **Judging black coffee by a bad cup.** Try a smooth bean brewed properly before deciding.
