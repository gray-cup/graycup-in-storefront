A strong filter coffee comes from three things: more Robusta in the blend, a thicker decoction, and less dilution with water or milk. Roast level barely changes the caffeine - it's the bean and the brew.

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## Brewing a Strong Decoction

1. Use **3 tablespoons of powder per cup**, pressed firm with the tamper.
2. Fill the top chamber with water just off the boil, cover.
3. Let it drip fully - 15-25 minutes. A rushed decoction is thin and weak.
4. Mix roughly **1 part decoction to 2 parts hot milk** (less milk than usual), and go easy on sugar so the strength comes through.

## A Note on Caffeine

Around 400 mg a day is the common safe ceiling for healthy adults - a couple of strong 30 g Robusta-heavy filter cups gets you there. Past that it's jitters, not more energy.
