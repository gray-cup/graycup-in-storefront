Espresso concentrates everything - flavour, body, acidity and defects. For a forgiving home shot you want a fresh, bold, medium-dark roast, and often a little Robusta for crema and a fuller mouthfeel. Very light, acidic roasts can pull sharp and thin on a home machine without a lot of dialling in.

## What Works for Home Espresso

- **Medium-dark roast** - sweeter, rounder, more forgiving under pressure.
- **A little Robusta (10-30%)** - thicker crema, more body, more caffeine. Optional but classic.
- **Fresh - within 3 weeks of roast**, rested at least 4-5 days after roasting.
- **Fine grind, ~1:2 ratio (18 g in, 36 g out) in 25-32 seconds.** Adjust grind to hit the time.

## Milk Drinks

For flat whites and cappuccinos, lean darker and toward more Robusta - the coffee needs to cut through milk. For straight shots, a cleaner medium-dark Arabica-forward roast tastes better.
