Starbucks India (run locally as Tata Starbucks) carries a fairly wide latte lineup, and it's easy to lose track of which is which - the classic caffe latte, several flavoured versions, iced versions, and a lighter "blonde" espresso option. Here's an overview of the range, plus how prices are generally structured (menus, sizes, and pricing vary by city and store, and change over time, so always check the current app or in-store menu for exact numbers).

## The Core Starbucks Latte

The **caffe latte** (sometimes written "café latte") is Starbucks' base latte: espresso shots (more shots in bigger sizes) topped with steamed milk and a thin foam layer. It's the least sweet option on the latte menu and the closest thing to what "latte" means outside of Starbucks-specific branding.

## Flavoured Lattes

Starbucks typically rotates and maintains several flavoured lattes built on the same caffe latte base with syrup added:

- **Vanilla latte / vanilla bean latte** - vanilla syrup or vanilla bean-flavoured syrup stirred through the espresso and milk.
- **Hazelnut latte** - hazelnut syrup, a nuttier, slightly less sweet-tasting option than vanilla or caramel.
- **Caramel-based lattes** - caramel syrup, often with a caramel drizzle finish.
- **Seasonal flavours** - limited-time syrups (like pumpkin spice) that rotate in and out depending on the time of year; see our [Pumpkin Spice Latte guide](/guides/starbucks-seasonal-drinks-india) for that one specifically.

## Blonde Espresso Lattes

"Blonde" refers to the espresso roast, not the milk or flavour - it's a lighter roast espresso than Starbucks' standard (Signature) roast, giving a milder, slightly more acidic and less bitter base. A **blonde vanilla latte** is simply a vanilla latte made with the blonde espresso shot instead of the standard one - same syrup, different bean roast underneath.

## Iced and Cold Lattes

Any of the above can typically be ordered iced - poured over ice with cold milk instead of steamed milk. An **iced latte** or **iced caffe latte** follows the same base recipe as the hot version, just built cold, and iced flavoured versions (iced vanilla, iced caramel, etc.) work the same way. A "cold latte" on the menu usually just means the iced preparation of whichever flavour you choose, rather than a separate drink category.

## Sizes and Roughly What Lattes Cost

Starbucks India sizes typically run Short, Tall, Grande, and Venti, with price increasing at each size step and flavoured or specialty syrups usually adding a small premium over the plain caffe latte. As a rough sense of positioning, hot lattes in India commonly fall somewhere in the **₹250-₹450 range** depending on size, and iced versions are often priced slightly higher than the equivalent hot size. These are general ranges, not fixed prices - actual pricing depends on the city, the specific store, and when you're ordering, and Starbucks updates its menu and pricing periodically. For exact, current pricing, check the Starbucks India app or the menu board at your local store rather than relying on any number you read online.

## Prefer to Make One at Home?

If you like the idea of a caffe latte but don't want to depend on a café run every time, the same drink is easy to approximate with a moka pot or a strong filter coffee decoction plus steamed or frothed milk - see our [What Is a Latte?](/guides/what-is-a-latte) guide for the method. Starting with a good bean matters more at home than it does at a café, since you don't have a machine doing the heavy lifting.
