Let's be straight: no coffee targets belly fat. You can't spot-reduce fat from one area by drinking anything. What black coffee *can* do is make an overall calorie deficit - the only thing that reduces belly fat - a bit easier to maintain: it's nearly calorie-free, mildly suppresses appetite, and slightly raises fat oxidation before exercise.

## How to Use Coffee as Part of Fat Loss

- **Replace calorie drinks with black coffee.** Swapping a 150 kcal latte or sugary cold coffee for black coffee is the real win.
- **Use it before a walk or workout.** Caffeine improves endurance and perceived effort, so you move more.
- **Keep it black.** Sugar and cream undo the point.
- **Don't over-caffeinate.** More than 3-4 cups won't burn more fat and will hurt your sleep, which raises appetite hormones.

## What Actually Reduces Belly Fat

A sustained calorie deficit, enough protein, resistance training, sleep and time. Coffee is a small assist on top of that - useful, cheap, but not the mechanism.
