Black coffee - just coffee and water, no milk or sugar - has a genuinely good evidence base for a few things and a lot of hype for others. Here's the honest split. None of this is medical advice.

## Well-Supported Benefits

- **Alertness and focus.** Caffeine blocks adenosine, improving reaction time, vigilance and short-term recall. Reliable and immediate.
- **Exercise performance.** A pre-workout cup improves endurance, power output and how hard effort feels - one of the most replicated findings in sports science.
- **Near-zero calories.** ~2-5 kcal per cup, so it's a good swap for sugary drinks when managing weight.
- **Appetite.** A mild, short-lived reduction in hunger.

## Associated (Population Studies, Not Proof)

- Lower risk of **type 2 diabetes**.
- Lower rates of **fatty liver** and liver fibrosis.
- Lower **all-cause mortality** in moderate drinkers.
- Possible links to lower risk of Parkinson's and some liver cancers.

These come from observational data - people who drink moderate coffee tend to do better, but the studies can't prove coffee is the cause.

## Overstated

- "Boosts metabolism to burn fat" - real but small (a few dozen calories) and fades with tolerance.
- "Detoxes" or "cleanses" - no.
- "Fat-burning coffee" products - the coffee isn't doing anything a normal black coffee wouldn't.

## Get the Benefits

Drink it black, 2-4 cups, before mid-afternoon, and pair the pre-exercise cup with actual movement.
