You don't need to spend big for good coffee. What actually matters at any price is that it's freshly roasted, cleanly processed, and brewed properly. Under ₹500 you can get 250 g of genuinely good Indian coffee - enough for roughly 15-25 cups.

## What to Prioritise on a Budget

- **Freshness over origin story.** A fresh commercial blend beats a stale "premium" bag.
- **Blends over rare single estates.** Blends are cheaper and more consistent.
- **Filter powder or ground blends** if you don't own a grinder yet.
- **Buy 250 g at a time** so it stays fresh.

## Make Cheap Coffee Taste Better

- Grind fresh if you can, or buy small ground packs.
- Water at 90-94 C, not boiling.
- Weigh your dose: 1:15 to 1:16.
- Store it airtight, away from heat and light.
