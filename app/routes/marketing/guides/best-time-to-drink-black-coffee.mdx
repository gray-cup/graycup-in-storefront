When you drink black coffee changes how well it works and how much it costs you in sleep. Caffeine peaks in your blood 30-45 minutes after a cup and has a half-life of roughly 5-6 hours - meaning a 4 pm coffee still has a quarter of its caffeine in you at 10 pm.

## Best Times

- **Mid to late morning (9:30-11:30 am).** Cortisol is already high right when you wake, so a cup an hour or two later gives a cleaner lift and may reduce tolerance build-up. This is the single best slot for most people.
- **30-60 minutes before exercise.** Caffeine improves endurance, power and perceived effort - time it so it peaks as you start.
- **Early afternoon dip (1-3 pm).** A small cup here fights the post-lunch slump without ruining your night.

## Times to Avoid

- **The moment you wake.** It works, but you're partly masking grogginess that fades on its own, and it can steepen the mid-morning crash.
- **After ~2 pm if you're caffeine-sensitive, after ~4 pm for most people.** Later than that and sleep quality drops even if you fall asleep fine.
- **On a completely empty stomach if it upsets you** - pair it with breakfast.

## For Specific Goals

- **Weight loss:** a cup before a morning walk or workout.
- **Focus for study/work:** a moderate cup every 3-4 hours, last one by mid-afternoon.
- **Night shift:** front-load at the start, small top-ups, none in the last few hours before you plan to sleep.
