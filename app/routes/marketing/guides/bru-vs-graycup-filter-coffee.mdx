Bru is one of the most recognisable coffee names in India, primarily known for instant coffee, but it also sells a dedicated filter coffee line - Bru Green Label - aimed at the traditional South Indian filter/decoction market. If you're searching for "green label coffee" or "green label filter coffee," this is usually the product people mean. Here's how that positioning compares to Gray Cup's filter coffee.

## Where Bru Green Label Sits

Bru's core brand strength is instant coffee, sold at massive national scale, and Bru Green Label extends that reach into the filter coffee category with a packaged blend meant to be widely available at an accessible price point. As with most large-scale filter coffee products, big national brands typically lean toward a chicory-inclusive or Robusta-forward ratio, since that supports a thicker, stronger decoction that holds up consistently across huge production volumes - a pattern typical of the mass-market segment rather than a specific claim about Green Label's exact formulation, which isn't publicly broken down.

## Where Gray Cup Sits

Gray Cup isn't trying to compete on shelf presence - it's a small-batch, roast-to-order model where the ratio is a choice, not a fixed spec:

- **Format** - a dedicated filter coffee blend from a brand built around filter coffee, rather than a filter line sitting alongside an instant-coffee-first catalogue.
- **Custom ratio** - choose between Arabica-forward blends, a classic 70:30 Arabica:Robusta, or a chicory blend, instead of one fixed nationwide formulation.
- **Roast freshness** - ground close to your order date rather than moving through long retail distribution cycles.

## Which One Fits You

Bru Green Label makes sense if you want a filter coffee that's easy to find anywhere and don't mind a fixed blend from a brand best known for instant coffee. Gray Cup makes more sense if you want a blend built specifically as filter coffee, with a ratio and roast date you control. Both serve the same broad decoction-and-milk ritual - the difference is standardisation versus customisation.
