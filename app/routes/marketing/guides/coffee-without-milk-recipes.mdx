**What is coffee without milk called?** Most simply, black coffee. Made from espresso diluted with hot water instead of milk, it's usually called an Americano. Beyond that naming, there's a fair amount of room to make milk-free coffee interesting rather than just "coffee minus milk" - this guide is six concrete ways to do that. For the bigger picture on why people cut milk in the first place, see [Coffee Without Milk: The Complete Guide](/guides/coffee-without-milk-guide) - this page sticks to recipes rather than repeating that background.

## 1. Classic Black Coffee

The baseline: brew your coffee as normal (drip, filter, French press, or pour-over) and drink it as-is. Use a slightly less bitter blend if you're new to going milk-free - lighter roasts and higher-Arabica blends tend to be smoother without milk to soften them.

## 2. Americano-Style

Brew a shot of espresso (or a small, strong batch of coffee) and top it up with hot water - roughly one part coffee to two or three parts water, adjusted to taste. This is the closest milk-free equivalent to a diluted milk drink in terms of volume and gentleness.

## 3. Frothy Black Coffee (No Dairy)

You don't need milk to get foam. Whisk a small amount of strong hot coffee vigorously with a spoon, hand whisk, or handheld frother for 20-30 seconds until a light foam forms on top, then pour the rest of your coffee underneath it. This "frothy black coffee" trick works because vigorous whisking traps air in the coffee's natural oils, similar to how a Vietnamese-style whisked coffee gets its top layer.

## 4. Sweetened Black Coffee

If black coffee tastes too sharp on its own, sugar (or jaggery, or a sugar syrup for easier mixing into cold coffee) solves that without touching the milk question. Start with a small amount and adjust - the goal is to round off bitterness, not mask the coffee entirely.

## 5. Iced Black Coffee

Brew double-strength (see our full [iced coffee recipe](/guides/best-ground-coffee-for-iced-coffee) for the method) and pour over a full glass of ice. No milk needed - the strength comes from the brew, not from dairy fat softening the flavour.

## 6. Oat or Almond Milk Black Coffee

If what you actually want is creaminess rather than dairy specifically, a splash of oat or almond milk gets you texture without cow's milk. It's not strictly "coffee without milk" in the plant-based sense, but it answers the common search for a creamy, non-dairy option and is worth including if lactose or dairy (not milk texture generally) is the actual reason you're avoiding it.

## Quick FAQ

**Is coffee without milk healthier?** It has fewer calories than a milk-heavy drink, but "healthier" depends on the rest of your diet - see [Black Coffee Benefits](/guides/black-coffee-benefits) for a fuller, more qualified answer.

**What's the difference between black coffee and an Americano?** Black coffee usually refers to drip or filter coffee taken plain; an Americano specifically starts as espresso diluted with hot water. Both are "coffee without milk."
