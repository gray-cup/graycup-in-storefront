Black coffee helps with weight loss in two small, well-documented ways: a cup has almost no calories (2-5 kcal, versus 100+ once you add milk and sugar), and the caffeine gives a modest, short-lived bump to metabolic rate and fat oxidation. It also blunts appetite slightly for an hour or two. None of this is dramatic on its own - it works because it replaces higher-calorie drinks and makes a calorie deficit a little easier to hold.

The catch is that this only works if you actually drink it black. A coffee that's too harsh to enjoy without sugar defeats the purpose, so the real job is picking beans that taste good with nothing added.

## What to Look For

- **Smooth, low-bitterness beans.** You want a cup you'll finish black, every day.
- **Ground, freshly brewed - not instant.** More of the chlorogenic acids and polyphenols survive.
- **No sugar, no cream, no "bulletproof" butter.** Those add back the calories you're trying to cut.
- **2-3 cups a day, earlier in the day.** More doesn't help; late caffeine costs you sleep, which itself works against weight loss.

## How to Use It

Drink your first cup 30-60 minutes before a workout or a walk - caffeine improves endurance and perceived effort, so you move a bit more. Keep total intake to 2-3 cups, all before mid-afternoon. Don't count on coffee to do the work: it supports a calorie deficit, it doesn't create one.
