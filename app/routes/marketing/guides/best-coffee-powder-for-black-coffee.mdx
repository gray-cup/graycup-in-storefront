If you don't own a grinder, the coffee powder you buy for black coffee has one job: taste good with nothing added. That rules out most cheap, dark, Robusta-heavy powders - they're built to survive milk and sugar, and drunk black they're harsh.

## What to Buy

- **Arabica or high-Arabica** - Robusta is about twice as bitter.
- **Medium roast** - dark roasts add roasty bitterness milk normally hides.
- **Grind to match your brewer** - fine for a steel filter, medium for drip or press.
- **Small, fresh packs** - ground coffee is at its best within 2-3 weeks.

## Brewing Black From Powder

- **Steel filter:** 2-3 tbsp powder, water just off the boil, 15-20 min drip, dilute the decoction ~1:2.
- **French press:** 15 g per cup, 240 ml at 94 C, 4 minutes, press and pour out.
- If it's bitter, use less powder or cooler water; if weak, use more or steep longer.
