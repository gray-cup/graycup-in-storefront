Beans that work with milk don't always work black. Milk hides bitterness, harshness and staleness; black coffee exposes all three. Beans that are genuinely good black share four traits: mostly or all Arabica, a clean process, a medium (not dark) roast, and freshness.

## What to Buy

- **Species:** Arabica, or a high-Arabica blend. Skip heavy Robusta - it's about twice as bitter.
- **Process:** washed or honey. Cleaner and more defined than a heavy natural.
- **Roast:** medium. Dark roasts add roasty bitterness that milk normally covers.
- **Freshness:** buy whole beans, use within 3 weeks of roast, grind just before brewing.

## Grind and Brew

- **French press:** coarse grind, 1:15, 4 minutes.
- **Pour over:** medium grind, 1:16, 3 minutes.
- **Water:** 90-96 C, never a rolling boil.
- **Grind fresh.** With black coffee it's the single biggest quality difference.
