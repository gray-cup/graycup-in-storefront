Filter coffee you'll drink every morning at home has slightly different priorities from a one-off special bag: it should be forgiving to brew, consistent batch to batch, good with the amount of milk you actually use, and priced so you don't wince refilling it.

## What Makes a Good Daily Home Filter Coffee

- **A forgiving blend.** Something that still tastes fine if your decoction runs a bit long or short.
- **Enough body for milk.** Most home filter coffee is drunk with plenty of milk, so a little Robusta or chicory helps.
- **Fresh, fine, consistent grind** - so every morning's decoction behaves the same.
- **Sensible pack size.** 250-500 g at a time; filter powder goes stale faster than beans.

## Daily Routine Tips

- Keep the powder airtight, away from the stove and sunlight.
- Rinse and dry the filter after each use - old grounds go rancid and taint the next cup.
- Warm the tumbler and davara with hot water first; it keeps the coffee hot through the frothing.
