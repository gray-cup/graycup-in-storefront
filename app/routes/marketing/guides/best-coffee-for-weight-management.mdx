Weight management is about keeping your calorie intake and output roughly balanced over time. Black coffee is a small, low-effort tool for that: it's essentially calorie-free, mildly curbs appetite, and makes exercise a little easier - so it helps at the margins without being a "diet" anything.

## How Black Coffee Helps

- **Calorie-free swap.** Replacing sweetened coffees, cold coffees and milky café drinks with black coffee removes 100-250 kcal a serving.
- **Appetite.** A mild, short-lived reduction in hunger between meals.
- **Exercise.** A cup before a walk or workout improves endurance and perceived effort.
- **Routine.** A consistent morning black coffee is an easy anchor habit while you build others.

## What It Won't Do

Coffee doesn't "boost metabolism" enough to matter long term, doesn't burn fat, and won't offset a diet that's over your calorie needs. It's an assist, not a lever.

## Keep It Simple

Drink it black, 2-3 cups before mid-afternoon, one before movement. Everything that actually manages weight - portion sizes, protein, sleep, activity - still has to happen alongside it.
