After freshness, grind size is the biggest lever on how coffee tastes. Finer grounds have more surface area, so they extract faster - too fine for your method and the cup turns bitter and harsh; too coarse and it's weak and sour. Each brew method has a window.

## Grind by Method

| Method | Grind | Like | Brew time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cold brew | Extra coarse | Peppercorns | 12-24 hrs |
| French press | Coarse | Sea salt | 4 min |
| Pour over (V60) | Medium | Table salt | 2.5-3.5 min |
| Drip machine | Medium | Table salt | 4-6 min |
| South Indian filter | Fine | Caster sugar | 15-25 min drip |
| Moka pot | Fine-ish | Between filter and espresso | 3-5 min |
| Espresso | Very fine | Powdered sugar / flour | 25-32 sec |

## Reading the Cup

- **Bitter, harsh, dry finish** = over-extracted → grind **coarser** (or cooler water, shorter brew).
- **Sour, thin, weak, salty** = under-extracted → grind **finer** (or hotter water, longer brew).
- Change one variable at a time.

## Grind Size by Brew Method, Quick Reference

| Brew method | Grind size | Texture |
|---|---|---|
| Ground coffee for cold brew | Extra coarse | Like peppercorns |
| Coarse ground coffee for cold brew | Extra coarse | Like peppercorns |
| Ground coffee for french press | Coarse | Like sea salt |
| Coarse ground coffee for french press | Coarse | Like sea salt |
| Grinding for aeropress | Medium-fine (adjustable) | Between table salt and caster sugar, depending on brew time |
| Ground coffee for moka pot | Fine-ish | Between filter grind and espresso |
| Ground coffee for espresso machine | Very fine | Like powdered sugar / flour |

Aeropress is the flexible one - a shorter, more espresso-like brew wants a finer grind, while a longer steep works better closer to pour-over medium. Start medium-fine and adjust from there using the same over/under-extraction cues below.

## Pre-Ground vs Grinding Fresh

Pre-ground coffee is fixed at one grind - fine if it matches your method, a compromise otherwise, and it stales within days. A burr grinder (not a blade one, which produces uneven dust) is the upgrade that improves every method at once. Until then, buy coffee ground for your specific brewer.
