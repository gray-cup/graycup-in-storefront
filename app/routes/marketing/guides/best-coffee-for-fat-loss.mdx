Coffee doesn't "burn fat" in any way that matters. What black coffee does do - and it's genuinely useful - is make the one thing that causes fat loss, a sustained calorie deficit, easier to hold: it's almost calorie-free, it dulls appetite slightly, and a cup before exercise helps you train harder and move more.

## How Black Coffee Helps Fat Loss

- **Calorie swap.** Replacing a sweetened coffee or cold coffee (100-250 kcal) with black coffee is an easy daily cut.
- **Appetite.** A mild, short-lived reduction in hunger between meals.
- **Training.** Caffeine improves endurance, power and perceived effort - a better workout for the same willpower.
- **Fat oxidation.** A small rise, mostly meaningful when paired with exercise.

None of this works if you add sugar and cream, or if the overall calorie maths doesn't add up.

## What Actually Drives Fat Loss

A calorie deficit, adequate protein, resistance training, sleep and consistency over months. Coffee is a small, free assist on top - not the mechanism, and not a substitute for the work.
