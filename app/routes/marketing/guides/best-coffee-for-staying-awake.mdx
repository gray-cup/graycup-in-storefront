Night shifts, long drives, back-to-back deadlines - sometimes you just need to stay awake and functional. Caffeine is the most effective legal way to do it. The key is a high enough dose from a strong brew, timed so it's working when you need it, without so much that you're wired and useless.

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## How to Stay Awake on Coffee

- **Front-load, then top up.** A strong cup at the start, smaller cups every 3-4 hours. Don't wait until you're already fading.
- **Strong brew.** More grounds, less water. A weak cup won't cut it at hour six.
- **Nap + coffee.** If you can, drink a cup then take a 20-minute nap - you wake as the caffeine kicks in. It's the single most effective trick.
- **Know the ceiling.** ~400 mg a day for healthy adults. Past that: racing heart, nausea, no extra alertness.

## After the Shift

Expect a rebound crash once the caffeine clears and a rough night's sleep if you dosed late. Staying awake on coffee is a short-term tool - pay the sleep back as soon as you can.
