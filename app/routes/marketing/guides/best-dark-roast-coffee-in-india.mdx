Dark roast is taken well past second crack. The origin's fruit and acidity burn off and roast flavour takes over: smoky, bittersweet, chocolatey, with a heavy body and an oily surface. It's bold and consistent - the same cup every time - which is why it suits espresso, moka pot and strong milk coffee.

## What Dark Roast Is Good For

- **Espresso and moka pot** - forgiving under pressure, thick crema, low acidity.
- **Strong milk coffee** - the roast flavour carries through dairy.
- **People who dislike acidity** - dark roasts are the lowest-acid option.
- **A bold black cup** if you like it heavy and roasty.

It's not the choice for tasting origin character - that's mostly gone at this roast level.

## Brewing Dark Roast

Dark roasts extract faster and can turn ashy if pushed. Use slightly cooler water (88-92 C), a slightly coarser grind than you'd use for a medium roast, and don't over-brew. In espresso, a shorter ratio (1:1.5-1:2) keeps it sweet.
