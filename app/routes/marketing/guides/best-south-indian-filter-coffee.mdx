South Indian filter coffee - "degree coffee" - is a method as much as a drink: fine coffee packed in a two-part steel filter, near-boiling water dripped through slowly to make a thick decoction, mixed with hot milk and sugar, then pulled between a tumbler and davara for froth. The cup depends almost entirely on the powder.

## The Traditional Blends

- **70:30 Arabica:Robusta** - the classic. Aroma from Arabica, body and strength from Robusta.
- **80:20 with chicory** - the Kumbakonam / "filter coffee" cafe taste: smoother, thicker, mellow.
- **Pure Arabica** - cleaner and more aromatic, best with less milk.

## Making a Proper Decoction

1. 2-3 tablespoons of powder per cup, pressed level with the tamper - firm, not compacted.
2. Fill the top chamber with water just off the boil, cover.
3. Let it drip fully - 15-25 minutes. Rushing it makes a thin, sour decoction.
4. Mix ~1 part decoction to 3-4 parts hot milk, add sugar, and froth by pouring between tumbler and davara from a height.
