India is the only major producer that grows nearly all its coffee under shade, mostly in Karnataka, Kerala, Tamil Nadu, Odisha and the Northeast. That gives slower-maturing cherries and cleaner cups. "Best beans" depends on how you brew and what you like, so start with those two questions.

## Pick by Species

- **Arabica** - smoother, more aromatic, more acidity, less caffeine. Best for black coffee, pour over and French press.
- **Robusta** - stronger, more bitter, heavier body, roughly double the caffeine. Best in milk coffee, espresso blends and South Indian filter.

See [Arabica vs Robusta](/guides/arabica-vs-robusta) for the full comparison.

## Pick by Roast

- **Light / Medium** - keeps origin character and acidity. Good for single origins.
- **Medium-Dark** - balanced, forgiving, works with milk or black.
- **Vienna / French** - bold, roasty, low acidity. Good for espresso and strong milk coffee.

## Pick by Process

Washed = clean and bright. Natural = fruity and heavy. Honey / HSD = somewhere between, with more sweetness.

## Practical Tips

- **Buy whole beans and grind fresh** if you have a grinder - it's the biggest single upgrade.
- **Buy small, buy often.** Coffee is best 5-21 days off roast. A giant bag goes stale before you finish it.
- **Match grind to brew:** coarse for French press, medium for pour over, fine for filter, extra-fine for espresso.
