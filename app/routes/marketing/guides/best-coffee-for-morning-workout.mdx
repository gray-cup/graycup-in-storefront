An early workout is the hardest one to caffeinate for - you're barely awake and there's no time to sip a cup slowly. The move is a strong coffee the moment you get up, so it's peaking in your blood by the time you're warmed up (caffeine takes 30-45 minutes to peak).

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## The Morning Routine

- **Drink it immediately on waking**, black, while you get changed.
- **Make it strong** - one solid cup, ~3 mg caffeine per kg bodyweight. No time for two.
- **Keep it black.** Milk and sugar sit heavy when you train soon after.
- **If you train fasted**, that's fine - black coffee doesn't break it and may help fat oxidation.

Drink the coffee, then do your mobility/warm-up and commute. By the time you hit the first working set it's peaking. That's the ideal - the caffeine and the workout line up.
