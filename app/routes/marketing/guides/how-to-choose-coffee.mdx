Choosing coffee comes down to five decisions. Get these right and almost any bag will make a good cup.

## 1. Species: Arabica or Robusta

- **Arabica** - smoother, aromatic, more acidity, less caffeine. Best for black and filter/pour-over.
- **Robusta** - stronger, more bitter, heavier, ~2x caffeine. Best with milk, in espresso blends, and for South Indian filter.

Most people want Arabica or a high-Arabica blend. See [Arabica vs Robusta](/guides/arabica-vs-robusta).

## 2. Roast Level

- **Light / medium** - keeps origin flavour and acidity; best black.
- **Medium-dark** - balanced and forgiving; black or milk.
- **Dark (Vienna / French)** - bold, roasty, low acidity; espresso and strong milk coffee.

## 3. Process

Washed = clean and bright. Natural = fruity and heavy. Honey/HSD = sweet, in between.

## 4. Grind

Match it to your brew: coarse for French press, medium for pour over/drip, fine for filter, extra-fine for espresso. Whole beans + a grinder is the single biggest quality upgrade.

## 5. Freshness

Look for a roast date, not just a best-before. Coffee is best 5-21 days off roast. Buy 250-500 g at a time and keep it airtight, away from heat and light.
