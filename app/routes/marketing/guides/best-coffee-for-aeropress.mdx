The AeroPress is a small plastic plunger that brews with a mix of immersion and gentle pressure, through a paper (or metal) filter. It's fast, hard to mess up, easy to clean, and it makes a clean, low-bitterness cup somewhere between filter coffee and a weak espresso. It works with almost any roast, which is part of why it's so popular.

## What Works in an AeroPress

- **Any roast** - light for a bright, tea-like cup; medium-dark for a fuller, chocolatey one.
- **Medium-fine grind** - finer than pour over, coarser than espresso.
- **1:15 to 1:16 ratio**, or brew a small concentrate and dilute.
- **Water at 80-92 C** - the AeroPress is forgiving, and cooler water suits lighter roasts.

## Quick Recipe

1. 15 g medium-fine coffee, filter rinsed.
2. Add 230 g water at ~85 C, stir gently.
3. Steep 1.5 minutes, insert plunger, press slowly over 30 seconds.
4. Stop when you hear the hiss - don't force the last of it.
