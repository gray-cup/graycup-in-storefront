Black coffee is the simplest pre-workout there is. Caffeine reliably improves endurance, power and how hard training feels, and coffee delivers it with the same evidence base as a caffeine pill. It's also cheaper than any tub of pre-workout and has no proprietary blend to decode.

## The Timing

Caffeine takes 30-45 minutes to reach peak blood levels and stays elevated for 3-5 hours. So:

- **Endurance / cardio:** drink it 30-45 minutes before you start.
- **Lifting:** 45-60 minutes before your first working set.
- **Early morning sessions:** have it as soon as you wake; it'll be working by the time you're warm.
- **Evening sessions:** be careful - caffeine this late can cost you 1-2 hours of sleep. Use a smaller cup or an earlier one.

## The Dose

About 3 mg of caffeine per kg of bodyweight is the researched sweet spot - roughly 1-2 strong cups for most people. More than ~6 mg/kg mostly just adds jitters.

## Keep It Black

Milk and sugar right before training tend to sit heavy and slow you down. If you train fasted for fat loss, adding calories to your coffee breaks the fast. Black is the pre-workout move.
