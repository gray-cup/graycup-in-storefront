Chicory is a roasted root blended into filter coffee - most commonly in South India, at 20-30%. It changes the cup and, in small ways, the nutrition. This isn't medical advice, but here's the honest ledger.

## Potential Benefits

- **No caffeine.** Every gram of chicory replaces a gram of caffeinated coffee, so a chicory blend is a lower-caffeine cup - useful in the afternoon or evening.
- **Inulin (prebiotic fibre).** Chicory root is one of the richest sources of inulin, a fibre that feeds gut bacteria. The amount in a cup of filter coffee is small but real.
- **Gentler on the stomach.** Many people find chicory blends less acidic and less harsh than straight coffee.
- **Smoother, thicker cup** with less bitterness - and it stretches your coffee, so it's cheaper per cup.

## Downsides and Cautions

- **Too much inulin causes bloating and gas.** A normal cup is fine; drinking very strong, very high-chicory coffee all day can upset some people.
- **Less caffeine cuts both ways** - if you rely on the lift, a high-chicory blend won't deliver it.
- **Not for everyone in pregnancy** - some sources advise limiting chicory; check with a doctor.
- **Flavour is an acquired taste** if you're used to pure coffee.
