India is one of the world's top Robusta producers, and Indian Robusta - especially washed "parchment" Robusta - has a better reputation than the commodity Robusta used in cheap instant coffee. It brings more caffeine, a heavier body, a thick crema in espresso, and a stronger, more bitter flavour. It shines in milk coffee, espresso blends and South Indian filter.

Caffeine figures assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## When Robusta Is the Right Choice

- **Milk coffee** - it punches through milk where light Arabica disappears.
- **Espresso blends** - 10-20% Robusta gives crema and body.
- **South Indian filter** - the traditional 70:30 blend depends on it.
- **Maximum caffeine** - roughly double Arabica, for less money.

## Brewing Robusta Well

Robusta turns harsh if over-extracted. Use slightly cooler water (88-92 C), a slightly coarser grind, and don't over-brew. In espresso, keep it as a minority of the blend. With milk, it's very forgiving.
