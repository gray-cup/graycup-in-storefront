India is a major Robusta producer, which is good news if you're chasing caffeine. Robusta carries about **2,400 mg of caffeine per 100 g** of grounds against Arabica's **~1,300 mg** - close to double. A higher-Robusta blend means more caffeine per cup, full stop.

The figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup. Here's the ranking, highest first.

## Brewing for Maximum Caffeine

- Use a **1:10 to 1:12** coffee-to-water ratio (most people use 1:16).
- Brew a **concentrate** and drink it neat or barely diluted.
- For **filter coffee**, make a thick decoction and use less hot water and milk.
- **Longer contact time** extracts more - a full 4-minute press or a slow drip beats a fast one.

## Don't Overdo It

The usual safe ceiling is about 400 mg of caffeine a day for healthy adults - roughly 3-4 strong Robusta-heavy cups. Beyond that you get anxiety, a racing heart and broken sleep, not extra alertness.
