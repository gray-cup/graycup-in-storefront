If you don't own a grinder, pre-ground coffee for milk drinks needs to hit two marks: the right grind for your brewer, and enough strength and body to stand up to dairy. A light, delicate ground coffee gets lost the moment milk goes in.

## What to Buy

- **Grind:** fine for a South Indian steel filter; medium for a drip machine or plunger. Buy it ground to match.
- **Blend:** something with 20-30% Robusta, or a chicory blend, for body under milk.
- **Roast:** medium-dark carries through milk better than a light roast.
- **Pack size:** 250-500 g. Ground coffee stales faster than beans, so don't buy a kilo.

## Storing Ground Coffee

Airtight container, cool dark cupboard, away from the stove. Ground coffee is at its best within 2-3 weeks - after that it goes flat and you'll notice it even under milk.
