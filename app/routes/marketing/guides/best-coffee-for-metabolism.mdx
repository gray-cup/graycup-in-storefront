Caffeine gives a small but measurable bump to metabolic rate - studies put it around 3-11% for a few hours after a cup - and it modestly increases fat oxidation, especially before exercise. Black coffee delivers this with almost no calories. It's not a fat burner, but it's a genuine, free nudge in the right direction.

## What Actually Matters

- **Drink it black.** Milk and sugar add the calories the metabolic bump was supposed to help with.
- **Ground, freshly brewed.** More chlorogenic acid survives than in instant.
- **Before movement.** Caffeine plus a walk or workout raises fat oxidation more than caffeine alone.
- **2-3 cups a day, earlier.** More doesn't raise metabolism further; it just costs you sleep, and poor sleep lowers metabolic rate.

## The Honest Version

The metabolic effect of coffee is real but small - a few dozen calories a day, and it fades as you build tolerance. It works best as a habit stacked on top of the things that actually move the needle: a calorie deficit, protein, sleep and movement.
