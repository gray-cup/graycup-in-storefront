Coffee isn't inherently bitter. Most of the bitterness people dislike comes from three things: too much Robusta, a dark or burnt roast, and over-extraction during brewing. Fix those and coffee tastes sweet, nutty and smooth - even black.

## Where Bitterness Comes From

- **Robusta.** Roughly twice as bitter as Arabica. Cheap blends use a lot of it.
- **Dark roast.** Roasting past a point adds scorched, ashy bitterness.
- **Over-extraction.** Water too hot, grind too fine, brew too long, or stale beans.

## Brew to Reduce Bitterness

- **Grind coarser** - the most common fix.
- **Cooler water** - 88-92 C, never boiling.
- **Shorter brew** - stop a pour over at 3 minutes, a press at 4.
- **Less coffee** if it's too strong (strength reads as bitterness).
- **Fresh beans** - stale coffee always tastes harsher.
- **Filtered water** - hard or chlorinated water roughens the cup.
