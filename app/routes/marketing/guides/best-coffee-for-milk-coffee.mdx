Milk mutes acidity and bitterness and adds sweetness and body. That means a coffee that tastes great black can disappear entirely once you add milk - too delicate. Coffee for milk needs strength and body to cut through: some Robusta, a darker roast, or a heavier natural process.

## What Works With Milk

- **Some Robusta** - 20-30% adds body, crema and a stronger backbone.
- **Medium-dark to dark roast** - the roast flavour carries through milk where bright notes don't.
- **Natural or honey process** - heavier, sweeter, more present under dairy.
- **A stronger brew** - make the coffee concentrated, then add milk.

## Ratio

For a South Indian style cup, roughly 1 part strong decoction to 3-4 parts hot milk, sweetened to taste. For a latte-style drink, a strong 1:2 shot topped with steamed milk.
