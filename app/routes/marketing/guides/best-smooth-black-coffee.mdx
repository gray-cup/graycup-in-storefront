## How to Keep It Smooth in the Cup

- **Grind coarser.** Fine grinds over-extract and turn harsh.
- **Cooler water.** 90-93 C is plenty; boiling water scorches.
- **Shorter brew.** Pull a pour over at 3 minutes, a press at 4.
- **Fresh beans.** Staleness reads as harsh and papery.
- **Filtered water.** Hard, chlorinated water dulls and roughens the cup.
