Instant coffee is brewed coffee that's been dried into soluble granules - you add water and it dissolves. Filter coffee is freshly brewed each time, whether South Indian decoction, pour over or drip. Both are real coffee; they differ on freshness, flavour, effort and cost.

## Filter Coffee

- **Flavour:** fuller, more aromatic, more nuance. Freshly extracted each cup.
- **Compounds:** retains more chlorogenic acids and other polyphenols than instant.
- **Effort:** grinding, brewing, a filter to clean. 5-20 minutes.
- **Cost per cup:** lower for the coffee itself, higher in time and kit.
- **Caffeine:** depends on dose and brew; a strong decoction is high.

## Instant Coffee

- **Flavour:** flatter, more uniform, often slightly bitter or "cooked." Better than it used to be, still not fresh-brewed.
- **Effort:** spoon, water, done. Seconds.
- **Cost per cup:** higher per gram of coffee, but no equipment.
- **Caffeine:** generally a bit lower per cup than a strong filter brew.
- **Best for:** travel, office, camping, or when you genuinely won't brew.

## Switching From Instant

If instant is your habit, start with a chicory filter blend and a simple steel filter - it's the most forgiving setup, and the jump in aroma is immediate. Keep instant for travel.
