Black coffee is just coffee and water. Milk coffee adds dairy (and usually sugar). The difference isn't only taste - it changes the calories, how it sits on your stomach, and what you can taste in the cup.

## Black Coffee

- **Calories:** ~2-5 kcal per cup.
- **Taste:** you get the full flavour of the bean - acidity, bitterness, origin notes.
- **Best with:** clean, medium-roast Arabica that isn't harsh.
- **Good for:** weight management, tasting single origins, anyone cutting sugar and dairy.
- **Downside:** a bad or over-roasted coffee has nowhere to hide.

## Milk Coffee

- **Calories:** ~50-150 kcal depending on milk and sugar.
- **Taste:** smoother, sweeter, rounder; bitterness and acidity are masked.
- **Best with:** bolder blends with some Robusta, or darker roasts, that cut through milk.
- **Good for:** people who find black coffee harsh, sensitive stomachs (milk buffers acidity), South Indian filter coffee.
- **Downside:** the added sugar is the part worth watching.

## The Honest Take

Neither is "healthier" by default. Black coffee is lower-calorie and better for tasting the coffee. Milk coffee is gentler and more comforting - the health question is really about the sugar you add, not the milk. Pick by preference, and go easy on the sugar either way.
