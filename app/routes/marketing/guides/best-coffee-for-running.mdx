A black coffee 45 minutes before a run is one of the cheapest, most reliable ways to run a bit better. Caffeine improves endurance and time to exhaustion, and it makes a given pace feel easier - which for most recreational runners is the bigger deal.

## The Runner's Version

- **Dose:** ~3 mg per kg bodyweight - one strong cup for most people.
- **Timing:** 45-60 minutes before you head out, so it peaks as you start.
- **Keep it black.** Milk and sugar before running is a recipe for a side stitch.
- **Mind the GI effect.** Coffee stimulates the gut; give yourself time for a bathroom stop before you leave.
- **Hot days:** coffee is fine and doesn't meaningfully dehydrate you, but it's not a substitute for water and electrolytes.

## Race Day

Practise your exact pre-race coffee in training first. Race morning is not the time to discover a new dose disagrees with you. If it works in training, keep it identical on race day.
