Cold brew steeps coarse grounds in cold water for 12-24 hours, then filters them out. No heat means far less acidity and bitterness extracted, so the result is smooth, sweet and mellow - and it keeps in the fridge for a week. Because the process already strips out sharpness, you want beans with flavour that survives it: chocolate, nut, caramel and low-acid profiles.

## What Works for Cold Brew

- **Medium-dark roast** - chocolate/nutty notes come through; bright, fruity light roasts get muted.
- **Coarse grind** - like French press. Fine grinds make it muddy and hard to filter.
- **1:8 concentrate ratio** - steep strong, then dilute with water, milk or ice to taste.
- **12-18 hours** at room temp, or up to 24 in the fridge.

## Quick Recipe

1. 100 g coarse coffee + 800 g cold water in a jar.
2. Stir, cover, steep 16 hours at room temperature.
3. Strain through a fine mesh, then a paper filter or cloth.
4. Dilute the concentrate roughly 1:1 with water or milk over ice.
