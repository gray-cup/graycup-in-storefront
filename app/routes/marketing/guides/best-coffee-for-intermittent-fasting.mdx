Black coffee is the fasting drink. It has 2-5 calories per cup - not enough to meaningfully raise insulin or break a fast for weight-loss purposes - and the caffeine can help in a few ways during a fasting window: it blunts appetite, lifts energy when you'd otherwise be flagging, and slightly increases fat oxidation.

## What You Can and Can't Add

- **Fine:** black coffee, plain. A pinch of salt if you like. Water.
- **Technically breaks a strict fast, but minor:** a tiny splash of milk (under ~20-30 kcal). Fine for most weight-loss goals, not for a clean autophagy-focused fast.
- **Breaks the fast:** sugar, honey, sweetened creamers, oat/almond milk lattes, "bulletproof" butter/oil coffee (that's 100-200+ calories).

## How to Use Coffee While Fasting

- **Have it mid-to-late morning**, when hunger tends to peak - it takes the edge off.
- **Don't overdo caffeine on an empty stomach** if it makes you shaky or nauseous. Start with one cup.
- **Watch total intake** - fasting can make you more caffeine-sensitive.
- **Stay hydrated** - coffee isn't a substitute for water, and fasting-day fatigue is often just dehydration.
