Caffeine is on every sports scientist's short list of things that actually work. It improves endurance, repeated-sprint ability, maximal power, and - maybe most usefully - it lowers rating of perceived exertion, so hard efforts feel easier. Coffee delivers it as effectively as a caffeine pill, and it's not banned (WADA removed caffeine from the prohibited list in 2004).

Caffeine figures below assume a strong 30 g dose of grounds per cup.

## How Athletes Should Use Coffee

- **Dose:** 3-6 mg per kg bodyweight. Most benefit is at the low end; higher just adds side effects.
- **Timing:** 45-60 minutes before the session or event.
- **Endurance events:** a top-up (1-2 mg/kg) late in a long race can help.
- **Tolerance:** keep habitual intake modest, or taper for 2-4 days before a key event, so race-day caffeine hits harder.
- **Test it in training first.** Never try a new dose on race day - GI issues are the classic mistake.
