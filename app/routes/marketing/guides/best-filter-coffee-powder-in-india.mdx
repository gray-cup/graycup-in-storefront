Filter coffee powder is just coffee ground fine enough for a South Indian steel filter - but the blend behind it decides everything: strength, bitterness, aroma, body and caffeine. Here's how the common options compare, with caffeine figures for a strong 30 g dose.

## The Blends, Compared

### Filter Coffee (Pure Arabica)

The cleanest, most aromatic powder. No Robusta, no chicory. Best with less milk so the aroma comes through.

## Which Should You Buy?

- **Want aroma over strength:** Pure Arabica or 95:5.
- **Everyday milk coffee:** 80:20 or 90:10.
- **Strong, traditional:** 70:30.
- **Classic South Indian cafe taste:** the 70:30 Arabica-chicory blend.

## Storing It

Filter powder goes stale fast because it's ground fine. Keep it airtight, away from heat and light, and buy 250-500 g at a time rather than a kilo.
