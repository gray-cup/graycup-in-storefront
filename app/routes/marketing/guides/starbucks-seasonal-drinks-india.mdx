The Pumpkin Spice Latte - almost universally shortened to **PSL** - is Starbucks' best-known seasonal drink. If you've searched for Starbucks pumpkin spice and found conflicting information about whether it's on the menu right now, that's because it genuinely isn't a year-round item anywhere Starbucks sells it, India included.

## What's Actually in a Pumpkin Spice Latte

A PSL is built like any other latte, with one addition:

- **Espresso** - the coffee base, same as a regular latte.
- **Steamed milk** - same ratio as a standard latte.
- **Pumpkin spice sauce/syrup** - a syrup flavoured with pumpkin spice notes (cinnamon, clove, nutmeg, ginger) rather than pumpkin itself in most formulations; the "pumpkin" is more of a seasonal flavour association than a literal pumpkin puree in every version.
- **Whipped cream and spice topping** - a dusting of pumpkin spice or cinnamon-sugar blend sprinkled on top of whipped cream, which is what makes the drink visually distinctive.

So a **pumpkin spice coffee** and a **pumpkin latte** are effectively the same drink referred to two different ways - the "latte" name refers to the espresso-and-milk base, and "pumpkin spice coffee" is shorthand for the same thing.

## Why It's Seasonal (and Why It Disappears)

Starbucks runs the PSL as a limited-time offer tied to autumn in most markets, and Tata Starbucks generally follows a similar seasonal window in India rather than keeping it on the year-round menu. Exact start and end dates shift from year to year and store to store, so if you're specifically hunting for the PSL, the most reliable thing to do is check the current Starbucks India app or ask at your local store rather than assuming it's available on any given date. Pricing also follows Starbucks' general specialty-drink range in India, which commonly falls somewhere in the ₹300-₹450 area depending on size and store - treat that as a rough guide, not a quoted price, since it varies and changes over time.

## Iced Pumpkin Spice Latte vs Hot

Both versions use the same pumpkin spice syrup and espresso base:

- **Hot PSL** - espresso, steamed milk, pumpkin spice syrup, topped with whipped cream and spice.
- **Iced pumpkin spice latte / cold pumpkin spice latte** - the same syrup and espresso poured over ice with cold milk instead of steamed milk, usually still finished with whipped cream if you ask for it.

Neither is objectively "the" PSL - it comes down to season and personal preference, and many people order the iced version even outside of hot weather simply because they prefer it.

## Home Version: Pumpkin Spice Latte Without Starbucks

You don't need the syrup or the seasonal window to make a close approximation at home.

**You'll need:** strong coffee (moka pot, espresso, or a strong filter coffee decoction), milk, pumpkin pie spice (or a mix of cinnamon, nutmeg, clove, and ginger), sugar or maple syrup, whipped cream (optional).

1. Brew 60-90ml of strong coffee.
2. Stir in 1/4 teaspoon of pumpkin pie spice and 1-2 teaspoons of sugar or maple syrup while the coffee is hot, so the spices bloom and dissolve.
3. Steam or froth 150-200ml of milk.
4. Combine the coffee and milk, top with whipped cream and an extra pinch of the spice mix if you want the full effect.

For an iced version, skip the steaming step and pour the spiced coffee and cold milk over ice instead.
