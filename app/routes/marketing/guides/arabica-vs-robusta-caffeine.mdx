Robusta uses caffeine as a natural pesticide, so the plant makes far more of it. In dry ground coffee:

- **Arabica:** about **1,300 mg per 100 g** (~13 mg per gram)
- **Robusta:** about **2,400 mg per 100 g** (~24 mg per gram)

That's close to double. The exact figure varies by variety, growing conditions and roast, but the gap is consistent.

## Other Things That Change Caffeine

- **Dose:** more grounds = more caffeine. This matters more than the bean at home.
- **Brew:** espresso is concentrated but small; filter and press cups are bigger. A large mug of filter coffee often out-doses a single espresso.
- **Roast:** dark roast has marginally *less* caffeine by weight than light - a small effect.
