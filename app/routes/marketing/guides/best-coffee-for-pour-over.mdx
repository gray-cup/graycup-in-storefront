Pour over (V60, Kalita, or any paper-filter dripper) is the most transparent way to brew: a paper filter removes oils and fines, so what's left is clarity and aroma. That makes it the best method for showing off a good single origin - and the least forgiving of a stale or over-roasted one.

## What Works for Pour Over

- **Light to medium roast** - pour over rewards origin character and acidity.
- **Washed-process Arabica** - the cleanest, most defined cup.
- **Medium grind** - like table salt. Adjust finer if sour, coarser if bitter.
- **Fresh beans, filtered water, 92-94 C, 1:16 ratio, ~3 minutes total.**

## Quick Recipe (V60)

1. Rinse the filter, add 20 g medium-ground coffee.
2. Bloom with 40 g water, wait 40 seconds.
3. Pour in stages up to 320 g total.
4. Aim to finish the drawdown around 2:45-3:15.
